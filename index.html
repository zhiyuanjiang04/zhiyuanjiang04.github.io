Research

I am interested in how agents can turn experience in the physical world into knowledge they can reuse, transfer, and build on. Looking ahead, I hope to pursue this question in three directions:

  • Reusable representations. Distill structure from human and robot experience to support perception and world modeling.
  • Transferable manipulation. Carry learned capabilities across tasks and embodiments.
  • Test-time improvement. Let agents refine their understanding and behavior through ongoing interaction.

Together, these directions point toward agents that learn from experience and become more capable as they act in the physical world.
